### What was announced

OpenAI [introduced GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/) as additions to the GPT-6 family. The September 22, 2026 update on its GPT-6 Astra announcement also links to this release.

### Availability and pricing

OpenAI says Sol and Luna are available in Codex and ChatGPT Work for eligible plans, and through its API as `gpt-6-sol` and `gpt-6-luna`. It lists GPT-6 Sol API prices at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. These figures are the company's published prices, not an independent comparison.

Read [OpenAI's announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/) and [model documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-sol) for current availability and limits.
