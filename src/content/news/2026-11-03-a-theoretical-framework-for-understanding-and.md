### Source-attributed summary

This summary uses the published excerpt from arXiv (cs.LG). It has not been independently fact-checked by Neural Pulse.

In this work, we provide a formal framework and mathematical definition for the subset of foundation models focused on enabling the property of &quot;any-task inference&quot; (ATI) (similar to zero-shot inference), rather than those designed for pre-training fine-tuning. To achieve this mathematical definition, we formalize the notion of a &quot;space of tasks of interest,&quot; which we include in the problem domain, then provide an interface-based formal definition for ATI foundation models as those that map samples of data $x$ and tasks $t$ to predictions over the space of labels directly. While simple, this definition allows us to show that all ATI foundation models can be decomposed through a &quot;universal task reduction&quot; lens into encoder, model, and decoder aspects.
