AI agents can assist scientific research, but balancing autonomous investigation with scientists&#x27; ability to inspect and redirect ongoing work remains a central design challenge. We present DiscoveryAgent, a domain-agnostic, scientist-centric system that combines autonomous investigation with scientist-directed hypothesis steering. Scientists select, refine, or replace hypotheses between research rounds, while audit agents interrogate checkpointed records to trace claims, reproduce analyses, and inform subsequent decisions. Across two predictive modeling tasks, DiscoveryAgent produced the best variable-star classifier among evaluated baselines and, with scientist steering, a protein variant-effect predictor surpassed by only one of 106 ProteinGym baselines. We develop a rigorous checkpointing system that enables highly capable audit agents, a scalable form of oversight for agentic research. Audit agents autonomously identify errors at higher rates than previous AI scientist agents, and scientists collaborating with audit agents identify 14x more valid errors versus scientists performing manual review.
