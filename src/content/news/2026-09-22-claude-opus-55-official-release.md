### What was announced

Anthropic [introduced Claude Opus 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5) on September 22, 2026. Anthropic describes it as the first model in its Claude 5.5 family.

### What the company reports

Anthropic says the model costs 40% less to run than Opus 5 on typical workloads in its own tests. It lists API prices of $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens. These are company-reported figures, not independent measurements by Neural Pulse.

Read [Anthropic's announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5) for availability, evaluation details, and limitations.
